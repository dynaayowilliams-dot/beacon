### Signed revocation and expiration
Expected: authorize only the original signing wallet, bind the exact target and original digest, keep the original entity unchanged and never infer active from lookup failure. Actual: separate revocation entity signed over protocol/domain, chain, project, action, key, original canonical EIP-191 digest, publisher, reason, timestamp and retention block. UI offers revocation only to a matching connected publisher; core rechecks account/network and exact original entity before signature/transaction. Readers independently revalidate original and revocation signatures, target key/digest and retention. A different entity creator or owner alone has no authority; correctly signed relayed revocations are accepted. No patch/delete operation is used.
Explicit Entity Expiration is notice's freshly read expiration block plus 302400 blocks (seven nominal days at two seconds/block), also signed. Both actual storage expiration and signed retention bound acceptance. Later notice/storage extension does not renew the signed revocation. UI separately displays original signature and Revoked by publisher, No verified live revocation found, or Revocation status unknown, with snapshot block and nonpermanence explanation. Lookup failure, snapshot mismatch or more than 20 pages without a valid positive yields unknown; expired/deleted records mean this is not a complete history. Timestamp tolerance is five minutes ahead of local clock.
